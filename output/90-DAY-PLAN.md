# Last Agency: the next 90 days

*Written from five live-SERP research passes run 26 July 2026. Every claim below traces to something a researcher actually fetched. Where something is unverified, it says so.*

---

## The strategic call

**Push published pricing backed by original price data, aimed at the buyer who is mid-purchase or mid-breakup with an agency. Not the guarantee, not city SEO, not AI/GEO.** Three independent lenses arrived at the same fact from different directions: no Indian agency of scale publishes a rupee retainer figure (Schbang, Dentsu India, Techmagnate, PageTraffic and Social Beat were curled directly — none has a rupee retainer number or a pricing link on the homepage; Techmagnate says in its own copy that pricing depends on scope), and every page ranking for Indian SEO cost queries invents its numbers — ten cost guides checked, zero citations, ranges contradicting each other by an order of magnitude, one still titled "What to Expect in 2024" and still ranking in July 2026. That SERP has no directories, no publishers, no Reddit, and literally has a Gumroad page and a Coursera user profile ranking in it. It is the only soft SERP this site touches. The obvious alternatives all fail on evidence, not on taste: **the guarantee is not a wedge** — BrainPulse, iMark Infotech, A New India and DigitalPRWorld all make identical or stronger money-back promises, and none of the agencies with real authority make one at all, so leading with it sorts you into the low-trust half of the market; **the 60 city pages are structurally dead** — Gurgaon, Bhubaneswar, Mumbai and Dubai SERPs are all review directories plus agencies with a physical address and a Google Business Profile, which are exactly the three things you don't have, and even tier-3 Bhubaneswar is saturated; **the AI/GEO cluster** looks winnable (the Google India top 8 for "how do i get my brand cited in chatgpt" is all unknown domains publishing within eight weeks) but that same fact means it re-sorts constantly and holding a position requires a two-week refresh treadmill a founder running an agency will not sustain; and **the 90 glossary and 150 answers pages** aimed at definitional terms are owned by Semrush, Ahrefs, Moz, Yoast and Backlinko — companies whose entire business model is publishing that content. One wedge, one audience, one asset.

## Weeks 1–2: the foundation that unblocks everything else

Nothing in Weeks 3–12 works until these exist. This list is deliberately five items long.

**1. Become a resolvable entity. Founder. ~4 hours.**
The site currently names no human anywhere. The only name discoverable on the entire domain is "priyanshu-semwal", leaking through the Cal.com booking URL. Put the founder's full name, photo, city and real bio on `/about`; add the registered entity name, address and CIN; add `Person` schema with `sameAs`. Create a personal LinkedIn profile and a company page the same day and link both from the footer.
*Unlocks:* Inc42 allocates one of its two permitted links to an author bio and bans authors by name for plagiarism — it tracks author identity. Social Samosa's eligibility copy is framed around people ("agency owner", "brand manager"). Clutch cross-checks business registration against India's Ministry of Corporate Affairs and displays it (PageTraffic's profile shows "PageTraffic Web-Tech Private Limited, Active status, India Ministry of Corporate Affairs"). Without this, every outreach channel below is closed. This is the gate.

**2. Fix the two credibility contradictions on `/pricing`. Developer. ~1 hour.**
The page lists an "Own Everything Stack" at ₹1,75,000/mo and separately states the range as "₹30,000–₹1,50,000 monthly" on the same page. The guarantee is worded two ways: "we work free until we do" in general and "the fee is free" for performance. Reconcile the range to the top bundle, pick one guarantee wording, state which service it applies to. Then put the full guarantee terms — baseline definition, measurement window, what qualifies, what happens on failure — in **one contiguous quotable block**. Perplexity retrieved the pricing page on a branded query and still hedged that "the search results I could access don't show the full" terms.
*Unlocks:* published pricing and the guarantee are your only two credibility assets; both currently contain an inconsistency a careful buyer checks before wiring ₹1.75 lakh to a three-week-old domain.

**3. Repoint the homepage's internal links. Developer. ~1 hour.**
The homepage is the only page on this domain carrying any authority, and all six of its deep content links currently go to pages that cannot win — `/glossary/backlink` competes with Wikipedia, Backlinko, Moz, Ahrefs, Semrush and Neil Patel in the top 7. Swap those six for: `/answers/can-i-fire-my-seo-agency`, `/answers/how-to-compare-seo-proposals`, `/journal/how-to-set-an-seo-baseline`, `/compare/retainer-vs-project`, `/journal/local-seo-in-tier-2-india`, `/cost/seo-cost-in-kolkata`. Keep the hub links.
*Unlocks:* the cheapest single change on this list. Every one of those six SERPs was checked and contains zero authority sites — micro-blogs and LinkedIn Pulse posts only.

**4. Claim free directory profiles. Founder. ~3 hours in one sitting.**
Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, Semrush Agency Partners. Identical name, address, phone and founder across all five. Put the actual rate card in the budget field rather than a range — that is the one field where your transparency is directly comparable. Turn on Sortlist's brief-matching ("Post a project for free and quickly meet qualified providers").
*Unlocks:* when Perplexity was asked which Indian SEO agencies publish pricing and offer a guarantee, 4 of its 10 sources were Clutch ranking pages and 1 was Semrush Agency Partners. For "who should I hire" questions the retrieval substrate is directories, not agency websites — no amount of on-site content puts you there. Sortlist's brief-matching is also a lead source that doesn't depend on ranking at all, which matters when you need three clients a month. **Do not pay for any tier** (see What NOT To Do).

**5. Sign three clients with published-results rights. Founder. Ongoing from day one.**
`assets/case-studies/` is an empty directory. Whatever price gets them signed, including free, with one non-negotiable written term: the right to publish the company name, the 90-day baseline number and the 90-day result. Also written into the engagement: a verified Clutch review and a named testimonial as a **contractual milestone at day 90**, not an afterthought.
*Unlocks:* everything downstream — Clutch ranking position (its India listings are ordered by review volume: WebSpero 120, PageTraffic 111, EvenDigit 104, SEOValley 38), listicle inclusion, trade-press case-study formats, award entries, and the proof that makes published pricing persuasive rather than merely unusual. This is the one asset that cannot be bought or written, only accrued, which is precisely why it's the market's moat.

## Weeks 3–8: the campaign

### Weeks 3–5 — build the asset

**The India Agency Price Index** (spec'd in full below). Founder owns the framing and the methodology; data collection is fully delegable.

While it's being built, **delegate three cheap content jobs**:

- **Add cited external sources to ~40 pages** — the `/cost/` cluster and the six Tier-1 buyer pages. Only 2 of 500 content files currently contain a single external link. The page that explains generative engine optimization discusses the November 2023 arXiv GEO paper by name, reports its finding that citations and statistics improve visibility in generated answers, and does not link to it. Add 3–6 named, dated, linked sources per page plus a visible "Sources" block and a last-verified date. Do **not** bulk-add to all 500.
- **Resolve the cannibalization.** Six pages target retainer-pricing intent (`/compare/retainer-vs-project`, `/compare/performance-based-seo-vs-retainer`, `/cost/hourly-vs-monthly-seo-pricing`, `/cost/seo-pricing-models`, `/answers/what-does-an-seo-retainer-include`, `/journal/what-a-75000-rupee-seo-retainer-actually-buys`). Pick `/compare/retainer-vs-project` as canonical; make it the only one the homepage and hubs link to; link the rest from it. Same treatment for the language pair (`/compare/english-vs-regional-language-seo` wins) and the offshore pair (`/compare/indian-seo-agency-vs-international-agency` wins).
- **Route the head term.** `/cost/seo-cost-in-india` is a top-of-funnel net, not a conversion page. The buyer typing that has been trained by page one to expect ₹5,000–₹15,000 — expertwebworld publishes Bronze at ₹5,000/mo, seoserviceinindia ranks with the literal title "Buy SEO Services @8000/- INR", thanksweb sets entry at ₹10,000–₹20,000. Your SEO floor is 9× the cheapest anchor. Point that page's internal links at the budget-qualified slugs: `seo-pricing-factors`, `seo-pricing-models`, `hourly-vs-monthly-seo-pricing`, `total-cost-of-seo`, `seo-agency-fees-breakdown`. Someone searching those has a budget.
- **Strip the map-pack promise** from the Chandigarh page ("Chandigarh SEO: a small map pack you can actually win"). Without an address you cannot have a Google Business Profile, so every local pack across all 60 city pages is categorically unavailable. Promising a map-pack win on a page that demonstrates no local presence is exactly what a sceptical buyer checks.

**Also delegate: render the marquee once.** Stripping tags from live HTML, the first ~700 characters of extracted text on every page is "Guaranteed — or it's free / The last agency you'll ever hire / No lock-in / Beat your baseline in 90 days / Only 3 clients / month" repeated six times, before the H1. Render once in the DOM, animate the repetition in CSS. Not a ranking fix — an extraction fix.

A live guest article was curled (HTTP 200, 593KB) and the literal string `nofollow` appears **zero times** in the entire HTML. Submit to **editor@inc42.com**.

Body: the ten ranking cost guides disagree by an order of magnitude and not one cites a source; here is what agencies actually publish, with the method stated. Two links — one to the Index methodology page, one in the author bio. **Do not mention the guarantee. Do not pitch the service anywhere in the body.** The promotional ban is enforced.

**Social Samosa — the one outlet that explicitly names agency owners as welcome contributors.**
Route is `socialsamosa.com/page/contribute-to-social-samosa` (the guessable `/contribute` 404s; the real URL is in the homepage footer). Eligibility copy: "It doesn't matter if you are a junior level executive, an agency owner, a brand manager or even a social media enthusiast." *What to send:* a genuinely different article from the Inc42 one — the exclusivity rule means a reslice will get both killed. Angle it at the social/performance side. Then, once the first cohort clears 90 days, come back with a social media case study, which is a format they list explicitly.

### Weeks 6–8 — Reddit and podcasts, at a deliberately small allocation

**Reddit: lead generation only, not links, and read the rules yourself first.** The researcher who tried could not load a single subreddit — curl, old.reddit and www.reddit all blocked — so every rule claim would be invention. Separately: across all seven India SERPs sampled, there was **not one** Reddit, Quora or mainstream-news result, including on an explicitly Reddit-framed query. India SEO SERPs do not have the UGC layer US SERPs do, and Reddit links are nofollowed as platform policy. The return here is brand searches, and brand searches are one of the two things this domain has zero of.

No public guest-pitch form or email was findable. Approach Akshay on LinkedIn with the data as the hook, not the agency as the subject. Low volume, slow; ranks below Inc42 and Social Samosa and should never displace them.

### The signals that tell you whether it worked

**Headline KPI for this whole period: indexed URL count in Search Console, charted weekly.** Indexation is the live bottleneck and it is already moving without intervention — a `site:` check on 27 July showed the hubs are out of "Discovered – not indexed" (`/answers` crawled 15 hours prior, `/seo-for` 8 hours prior) and at least one detail page, `/glossary/hreflang`, is in. Roughly ten URLs visible against 517 published, though a bot challenge blocked enumeration past page one, so treat that as a floor not a count. **If the curve plateaus well under 517, the problem is crawl budget on a no-authority domain — an off-site problem, which confirms the directory and link priorities above and means the answer is not more content.**

**Second: a saved Search Console filter containing only the twelve Tier-1 URLs**, reviewed weekly:

`/answers/can-i-fire-my-seo-agency` · `/answers/how-to-compare-seo-proposals` · `/journal/how-to-set-an-seo-baseline` · `/compare/retainer-vs-project` · `/journal/local-seo-in-tier-2-india` · `/compare/google-business-profile-vs-website` · `/journal/how-to-read-an-seo-report` · `/compare/da-vs-dr` · `/journal/seo-in-hindi-and-indian-languages` · `/compare/indian-seo-agency-vs-international-agency` · `/answers/what-does-an-seo-retainer-include` · `/cost/seo-cost-in-kolkata`

Every one of those SERPs was pulled and contains no Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, HubSpot, WebFX or Backlinko — sub-1000-visitor blogs and LinkedIn Pulse posts only. Eleven of the twelve serve the same buyer: someone mid-purchase or mid-breakup with an agency, which is exactly who a baseline guarantee is for. **Expect impressions on 4–6 of them within 3–6 weeks of indexation; clicks lag another 4–8 weeks.**

**Third: a second saved filter, reviewed monthly**, for the bench — `/answers/how-do-i-know-if-my-seo-agency-is-doing-a-good-job`, `/journal/the-real-cost-of-switching-seo-agencies`, `/answers/how-many-seo-agencies-are-there-in-the-world`, `/seo-for/exporters`, `/cost/seo-cost-for-small-business`, `/journal/what-a-75000-rupee-seo-retainer-actually-buys`, `/journal/why-free-seo-audits-are-sales-pitches`. **Fourth, ten minutes a month:** re-run three Perplexity queries verbatim and log the sources —
1. "What does lastagencyhere.com charge for SEO and what is their guarantee?" (baseline: 7 of 25 sources were lastagencyhere.com pages, and it correctly returned "from ₹75,000 per month" and "Rank or It's Free" — retrieval already works)
2. "What should I expect to pay an SEO agency in India per month in 2026, by band?" (baseline: 10 sources, **zero** from lastagencyhere.com, despite the site having a page literally titled "SEO pricing in India, band by band")

**Fifth, two hours, once:** capture the real India-geo SERPs. Every SERP judgement in this plan came from a US-geolocated tool or from DuckDuckGo `kl=in-en`, neither of which renders AI Overviews or map packs. Competitive *shape* reads reliably across both — Clutch, Semrush and DesignRush holding Mumbai's top three is not a geo artefact — but exact ordering and AI Overview citations in India are unverified. India-geo incognito, screenshot page one for the eight core queries, record what the AI Overview cites. Re-shoot every 90 days against the same window you sell.

**What will still be flat at week 12 and should not alarm you:** clicks, revenue from organic, anything in the glossary or career clusters, and all 60 city pages.

## The original-data asset

**The India Agency Price Index.** This is credible and it is the only original research this business can produce right now, because every input is public rather than client-derived. It also converts "we publish our prices" from a claim into a body of work.

⚠️ **Name check required before you commit.** The global rule is to verify a name isn't taken before treating it as final. My search budget was exhausted before I could check "India Agency Price Index" or "Indian SEO Price Index" against npm/trademark/existing publications. Spend ten minutes on this before you print it anywhere — "price index" is a common construction and there may well be a finance or martech product using it.

**What to collect** — one row per agency, every row carrying a source URL, a screenshot and a capture date:
- Published monthly retainer or package price in INR, where the agency publishes one
- Deliverables and keyword counts attached to each tier
- Clutch minimum project size and hourly band
- Sortlist / Semrush Agency Partners / DesignRush "starting from" and minimum-budget figures
- City and founding year

**From whom:** 100+ Indian agencies. The public sources already confirmed to carry usable numbers: Clutch's India SEO listings (minimum project size and hourly band on every profile), Semrush Agency Partners (Schbang $2,500, WebHopers $1,000, eSearch Logix $1,000–2,500), Sortlist ("From €1,000"), DesignRush (minimum budget plus hourly $10–150), and agencies' own package pages (expertwebworld ₹5,000/9,000/15,000/45,000, thanksweb ₹10,000–20,000, and the ten agencies in Bloom Agency's Mumbai table at ₹18,000–₹50,000). Add two proprietary columns nobody else can build: **proposals you receive or request**, and **what inbound leads say they currently pay**. Include Last Agency's own rates as one row among many — that is the credibility move, not a plug.

**How long:** one researcher fetched exact published INR figures from four agencies in roughly twenty minutes. 100 rows is realistically 12–15 hours of delegable collection plus 4–6 hours of founder work on methodology and write-up. Two to three weeks elapsed at agency pace.

**Who would cite it:** Inc42 and Social Samosa both require data-backed non-promotional content and both pass followed links — that's the primary target and it's why the Index exists. Downstream: Media Brief, Marketing Mind, then e4m / afaqs! / ET BrandEquity as a reporter's source. Note the pattern to copy — Techmagnate's link-earning assets are its "Digital Report Card (FY 2025-26)" built on "real data from 103 brands" and 20+ vertical trend reports. That's aggregated client data you can't replicate; public price data is the version you can.

**The headline finding it needs, to get picked up:** *the ten pages ranking on Google India for what SEO costs in India disagree with each other by an order of magnitude, and not one of them cites a source.* That's a story about the market misinforming buyers, not a story about you — which is why it clears Inc42's promotional filter. The supporting number is the real distribution of published prices with a stated sample size.

**Non-negotiables that make it citable rather than another agency blog post:** a stated methodology, a stated sample size, named and linked sources on every row, a visible capture date, a downloadable CSV, a public changelog, and a quarterly refresh on a stable URL. Every competitor asserts ranges; none evidences one. Bloom Agency built the closest thing — a ten-agency Mumbai price table — and published no methodology, no dates, no sources and no outbound links. Doing those five things is the whole moat.

## What NOT to do

**Do not touch, rewrite or expand the 60 `/seo-agency/` city pages.** Nine city SERPs were sampled across three competition tiers. All nine are review directories (GoodFirms, Clutch, DesignRush, Sortlist, SuperbCompanies) plus agencies with a local address, a Google Business Profile and a public client count. Mumbai's top three are Semrush, Clutch and DesignRush. Even tier-3 Bhubaneswar is saturated. The ranking currency is exactly the three things you don't have. Exclude them from the weekly review so their zeros don't distort your read. Revisit when one real GBP listing has 10+ genuine reviews — and then work **one** city, the one your office is actually in, not sixty.

**Do not buy a directory placement.** Clutch sponsored listings are reported at $1,500–$4,000/month and premium at $3,000–$7,500/month, sales-quoted, on annual contracts totalling $20,000–$100,000+/year for top categories. That is a client's entire retainer, paid monthly, for visibility. GoodFirms is reported from ~$300/month and DesignRush from ~$200/month on annual commitments. **The free profile is worth claiming; the paid tier is not, at three clients a month.** The reason to be on Clutch is the phone-verified review, not the placement. (Clutch and GoodFirms both returned 403, so whether their profile links are followed is genuinely unknown — treat any link value as unconfirmed upside.)

**Do not run a Reddit or Quora seeding programme.** Zero forum results across seven India SERPs, including on an explicitly Reddit-framed query. Reddit links are nofollowed regardless. This is the single most plausible-sounding month-burner on the list, because it's correct advice for US SERPs and simply doesn't describe this market.

**Do not chase inclusion in the "top SEO agency in India" listicles.** upGrowth's page ranking #1 for that query lists upGrowth first and contains **no outbound links to any agency it names** — the only links point at upGrowth's own services. CMARIX's "Top 11 SEO Companies in India" names eleven companies, ranks itself #1, and hyperlinks none of them. Bloom Agency's Mumbai listicle does the same. These are competitor lead-gen content dressed as editorial. Zero link equity, no submission route, and they rank themselves above you by construction.

**Do not submit to YourStory's PitchFest.** The only submission route findable on the site is a four-step investor pitch funnel via JotForm — deck submission, panel review, coaching, then a sub-3-minute live pitch to investors. It is for fundable startups, not a three-clients-a-month services agency. No public contributor route is discoverable; revisit only via a warm introduction to a named editor.

**Do not pursue Storyboard18 or ETBrandEquity yet.** 32 nofollow occurrences on the ET homepage versus zero on Inc42's and Social Samosa's, and no discoverable contributor route on either. Prestige mentions, not link targets, and neither will take a cold pitch from an unknown agency.

**Do not enter awards this quarter.** The e4m Indian Digital Marketing Awards 2026 deadline (8 June) has passed, and every open programme — IAMAI India Digital Awards, Campaign India Digital Crest — judges submitted campaign work. Structurally gated behind delivered client results. Diarise the 2027 windows now and build the first three engagements so they're documentable: baselines agreed up front, before/after captured, publication permission in writing.

**Do not commission another AEO or on-page GEO improvement.** Perplexity already retrieves seven of your pages on a branded query and quotes your price and guarantee correctly. Retrieval is solved; the constraint is candidate-set entry, and passage relevance does not beat index position. Every further hour on "citability" buys nothing.

**Do not maintain the 133 KB `llms.txt`.** Google's own documentation states "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup to appear in these features" and never mentions llms.txt; Gary Illyes confirmed in July 2025 that Google doesn't support it and doesn't plan to; Mueller compared it to the keywords meta tag. Leave the file, stop maintaining it, and **remove it from any sales material** — a prospect who checks will find Google's documentation contradicting you, and your one real claim is that you tell the truth about SEO. (The permissive robots.txt next to it is doing all the actual work — GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and CCBot are all allowed, which is why Perplexity could read you at all. Leave that alone.)

**Do not rewrite the four category-error pages.** `/answers/how-many-clients-should-an-seo-agency-take`, `/journal/what-clients-have-to-do-for-seo-to-work`, `/journal/is-your-agency-outsourcing-your-seo` and `/journal/where-your-seo-retainer-money-actually-goes` all target phrases the index has assigned to agency *owners* looking to acquire clients or outsource, not agency *buyers* — for two of them, ten of ten results are "how to get SEO clients". These aren't quality failures, they're intent mismatches, and no rewrite fixes it. One exception worth tracking: the secondary "how many clients does an seo account manager handle" returns Reddit and a Moz forum thread and is genuinely open.

**Do not mass-touch publication dates.** All 500 pages currently carry `published: 2026-07-26`, in tight per-family word-count bands (answers median 2,168, glossary 1,442, journal 3,158). That is already a recognisable scaled-generation fingerprint on a three-week-old domain with no brand signals. There are no manual actions and no evidence of a penalty, so this is a risk note, not a diagnosis — but a bulk date refresh would convert a passive risk into an active signal. Stamp an `updated` date only when a page genuinely changes, and concentrate real updates on the ~40 pages in the two winnable clusters so those specific pages develop honest recency while the rest stay honestly static.

**Do not benchmark against Yethi, Hashmeta, Wasserman, VSA Partners or VServ.** They are, respectively, a Bengaluru BFSI test-automation firm, a Singapore social agency, an LA sports and talent agency, a Chicago brand design shop, and a Mumbai mobile adtech platform. None sells Indian SEO retainers. The repo's own keyword brief already flagged them as unrelated entities and correctly declined to build pages on them — that call is right and should stand. Your actual benchmark set is Techmagnate, PageTraffic, Infidigit, upGrowth, ThatWare, WebSpero, EvenDigit, SEOValley and eSearch Logix.

**Do not lead with "guaranteed SEO" or "money back" as headline copy anywhere.** Demote the guarantee from lead position to a contract detail mentioned after proof, and reframe it from a refund promise to a **published measurement methodology** — what's measured, how the 90-day comparison is computed, what "work free" means contractually. An auditable method is rare and defensible; a refund promise is neither, and it puts you in a SERP with BrainPulse and DigitalPRWorld.

**Do not write a single new page** until the indexation curve's shape is understood. You have 500 pages and 10 visible in the index. Adding more is the least useful thing available.

## The honest expectation

**At 3 months: almost nothing in Search Console, and that is the correct outcome.** Realistically: impressions on somewhere between four and eight of the twelve Tier-1 URLs, probably in single-digit-to-low-double-digit daily impressions, and very possibly zero clicks. Indexed URL count somewhere between 60 and 250 of 517 — that spread is honest, because it depends almost entirely on whether links land, and nobody can narrow it from here. Zero revenue attributable to organic. If Inc42 or Social Samosa runs, one to three followed links from a starting position of zero. **The leading indicators that matter at 3 months are: indexed URL count rising week over week; the twelve Tier-1 URLs registering their first impressions; at least one directory profile live with at least one verified review; and the founder's name existing as a resolvable entity on LinkedIn.** If those four are true and clicks are still zero, the plan is working.

**At 6 months: the first real traffic, from a narrow set of pages, and it will look disappointingly small.** If the Index published and earned links, expect clicks in the tens per day, concentrated almost entirely in the `/cost/` family and the buyer-intent cluster — not spread across 500 pages. Six to twenty referring domains, most of them from two or three published articles plus directory profiles. The `/cost/seo-cost-in-india` head term will not be won; the qualified long-tail around it might start moving. First Clutch reviews live if the day-90 milestone was contractual. Perplexity query 2 may start returning your pages. **The honest risk at 6 months is that the Index gets built and nobody picks it up** — the mitigation is that the data is reusable for a second and third pitch, not that the first pitch is guaranteed to land.

**At 12 months: a small, real, defensible position — or a clear signal to stop.** The realistic good case is: the site is a recognised reference for Indian agency pricing, 30–80 referring domains, a Clutch profile with 6–15 verified reviews, organic contributing perhaps one of the three monthly clients, and a handful of `/cost/` and buyer-intent pages ranking on page one for mid-tail queries. **Not won at 12 months, and unlikely ever from here without a fundamentally different investment: "seo agency india", "seo services india", "best seo agency in india", "seo company in mumbai", any map pack anywhere, and every definitional glossary term.** Those are 20-year domains with hundreds of verified reviews and, in the glossary case, companies whose business model is publishing that content.

**The thing to internalise:** for the first quarter you are not doing SEO, you are manufacturing the entity and the proof that SEO needs in order to work — a named founder, a registered address, three named clients, verified reviews, and one piece of data worth citing. Content is not the constraint; you have 500 pages and no links. Judge the quarter on whether those five things exist, not on the traffic graph.
