## 5. Non-negotiable content rules

1. **Every page must earn its URL.** Unique angle, unique data, unique examples. No
   template-filled permutations — Google classifies that as scaled content abuse and it
   will suppress the whole domain, not just the thin pages.
2. **Answer first.** Every answer page opens with a 40–60 word direct answer suitable for
   a featured snippet, before any preamble.
3. **Brand voice.** Short declarative sentences. Contractions. Specific numbers. Cheeky,
   never snarky. See `output/brand-voice-guide.md` and `output/banned-phrases.json`.
   Hard-banned: elevate, unlock, synergy, leverage, transform, empower, best-in-class,
   end-to-end, world-class, cutting-edge, results-driven, thought leadership, journey
   (figurative), solutions (noun), "we believe", "drive growth".
4. **Honest about the guarantee.** We never promise a specific ranking position. We
   guarantee movement against the client's own baseline. Say so wherever the guarantee
   is mentioned.
5. **No competitor brand pages.** No "best SEO agency in X" list pages that rank other
   agencies, and no pages targeting another company's trademark.
6. **Pricing must match the site**: SEO from ₹75,000/mo (smaller sites from ₹40,000),
   Social ₹30,000–₹70,000/mo, Performance ₹40,000–₹1,50,000/mo. GST extra. Bundles:
   ₹99,000 / ₹75,000 / ₹1,75,000.
